📞 About the Product

Does it actually fill thinning areas or just darken the scalp?+
It does both. The micro-fibers in the spray attach to existing short hair and scalp skin, creating the appearance of a fuller, denser hairline. It doesn't grow hair — it fills the visual gaps so you look like you just had a fresh cut.
Will it look fake or obvious up close?+
No — the matte finish is specifically designed to match natural hairline texture. It doesn't shine or clump. Even close-up and in good lighting, it blends in completely. People only notice if you tell them.
Does it wash out completely?+
Yes — completely removed with one regular shampoo wash. No residue, no buildup, no staining of the scalp.
Will it rub off on my shirt collar or pillow?+
No. Once it dries (about 30 seconds), it is set. It does not transfer to clothing, pillows, or anything you touch. Hundreds of our customers wear it under formal shirts and to bed without any issues.
Will it survive Lagos heat and sweat?+
Yes — it is sweat resistant and heat resistant. Customers have worn it through full outdoor events, afternoon Lagos traffic, and long work days with no fading or smearing. Light drizzle is also fine — just avoid full submersion in water without reapplying.
How long does one bottle last?+
With daily use, one bottle lasts approximately 30–45 days (4–6 weeks). If you want longer coverage without reapplying daily, the Complete Set includes a Hair Stick that keeps your hairline set for 5+ days at a stretch.
Will someone feel it if they touch my head?+
No — there is no texture, no stickiness, no crunchiness. It feels the same as natural hair to the touch. Partners, barbers, and family members regularly do not notice unless told.

⚙️ How to Use It

How do I apply it correctly?+
Step 1: Shake the bottle 3–4 times. Step 2: Hold the nozzle 5–8cm from your hairline. Step 3: Spray in short, light passes — build up gradually. Step 4: Gently pat with your fingertip or a soft brush to blend. Wait 30 seconds to set. Less is always more — start light and add.
Can I use it on a very low cut or shaved head?+
Yes — it works on very low cuts and even completely shaved areas. Very short hair gives it the most realistic finish because the fibers match and blend with the short stubble perfectly.
