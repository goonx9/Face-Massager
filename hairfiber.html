📞 About the Product

Does it actually fill thinning areas or just darken the scalp?+
It does both. The micro-fibers in the spray attach to the existing short hair and scalp skin, creating the appearance of a fuller, denser hairline. It doesn't grow hair — it fills the visual gaps so you look like you just had a fresh cut.
Will it look fake or obvious up close?+
No — the matte finish is specifically designed to match natural hairline texture. It doesn't shine or clump. Even close-up and in good lighting, it blends in completely. People only notice if you tell them.
Does it wash out completely?+
Yes — completely removed with one regular shampoo wash. No residue, no buildup, no staining of the scalp. You start fresh every time.
Will it rub off on my shirt collar or pillow?+
No. Once it dries (about 30 seconds), it is set. It does not transfer to clothing, pillows, or anything you touch. Hundreds of our customers wear it under formal shirts and to bed without any issues.
Will it survive Lagos heat and sweat?+
Yes — it is sweat resistant and heat resistant. Customers have worn it through full outdoor events, afternoon Lagos traffic, and long work days with no fading or smearing. Light drizzle is also fine — just avoid full submersion in water (pool or heavy rain) without reapplying.
How long does one bottle last?+
With daily use, one bottle lasts approximately 30–45 days (4–6 weeks). If you have a larger area to cover, it may be closer to 30 days. For moderate coverage, closer to 45. That's why many customers get the Double Pack — you save and never run out.
Will someone feel it if they touch my head?+
No — there is no texture, no stickiness, no crunchiness. It feels the same as natural hair to the touch. Partners, barbers, and family members regularly do not notice unless told.

⚙️ How to Use It

How do I apply it correctly?+
Step 1: Shake the bottle 3–4 times. Step 2: Hold the nozzle 5–8cm from your hairline. Step 3: Spray in short, light passes — build up gradually, don't overspray. Step 4: Gently pat with your fingertip or a soft brush to blend. Wait 30 seconds to set before touching. Less is always more — start light and add.
Do I apply it before or after moisturizer?+
Apply it last — after your moisturizer or any face products. Your hairline area should be dry and product-free when you spray. Oily skin at the hairline can slightly reduce how long it lasts, so ensure that area is clean and dry.
Can I use it on a very low cut or shaved head?+
Yes — it works on very low cuts and even completely shaved areas. In fact, very short hair gives it the most realistic finish because the fibers match and blend with the short stubble perfectly.

❤️ Safety & Skin

Is it safe for sensitive scalp or skin conditions?+
Yes — dermatologist tested with no harsh chemicals, parabens, or known irritants. It is safe for daily use on all skin types. If you have an active scalp infection or open wound, avoid applying directly on the affected area until healed.
Will daily use cause my hairline to recede further?+
No — this product sits on top of the skin and hair. It does not affect hair follicles, hormones, or hair growth in any way. It is purely cosmetic. Daily use will not worsen or improve hair loss — it simply covers it.
